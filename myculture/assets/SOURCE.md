# Where the images come from

| File | Source | How it was made |
|---|---|---|
| `logo.png`, `logo-dark.png` (512 × 512) | `Happily-Assessment/src/assets/image/logo_myculture.svg` (the MyCulture logo used in the assessment app) | The donut mark only, cropped from the SVG's own viewBox (first 32 × 32 units), rendered with `rsvg-convert`, centred on a transparent square. Colours and shape unchanged. |
| `icon.png`, `icon-dark.png` (128 × 128) | Same | The 512 px logo scaled down. |

The dark variants are the same images: the mark has no dark-coloured parts and reads on both light and dark backgrounds. The wordmark (dark grey text) isn't used, because it would be unreadable on dark backgrounds and recolouring it would change the brand.
