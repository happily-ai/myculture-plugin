# Privacy

This plugin contains Markdown instructions (skills), JSON metadata and a connector address (`.mcp.json`). It bundles
no script, hook, installer or background process, and installing it runs no program.

The skills only guide how your assistant uses the MyCulture connector. Data moves only when your assistant calls a
MyCulture tool on your behalf, under the account and permissions you approved when connecting. MyCulture records
each tool call for the connected workspace. The connector masks candidate emails and doesn't return candidates'
raw answers.

Your assistant provider processes and may retain conversations and tool results under its own settings and terms.

The plugin is published by Happily.ai, the company behind MyCulture. Full privacy policy: https://www.myculture.ai/privacy-policy. Questions: https://www.myculture.ai/contact-us.
